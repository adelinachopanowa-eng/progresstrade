Who we are

Buying ferrous and non-ferrous metals since 2002

Progresstrade Ltd has been in the scrap metal trade since 2002, and the terms have stayed the same throughout: a calibrated weighbridge, documents under the Waste Management Act and payment by bank transfer.

The yard is a licensed waste treatment site and we hold a licence for waste transport. We issue every document needed for correct reporting and for compliance with environmental law, which matters as much to a foreign company with an audit ahead of it as it does to a local contractor.

Yard and equipment

Our yard and our equipment

We work with our own Sennebogen material handlers, dismantling equipment and tractor units with tipping trailers. Material is accepted, weighed, sorted and loaded on site, and for larger quantities we collect from your address. The yard has access for heavy goods vehicles and for rail wagons.

Site area 6,700 m²Truck scale 60 tRail weighbridge 120 tHydraulic shear 650 t/dayBaling press 200 t/dayContainers up to 30 m³CCTV 24 hoursPermitted intake up to 100,000 t/year

Both weighing devices are calibrated and every weighing is documented. The capacity figures are the rated throughput of the equipment and are indicative; what matters for your consignment is the quantity recorded on the weighing note.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

How long have you been trading?
Progresstrade has been buying ferrous and non-ferrous metals since 2002. The yard is in the Voenna Rampa district of Sofia.
Are you licensed?
Yes. We hold a waste treatment permit and a registration document for waste transport, and every transaction is documented under the Bulgarian Waste Management Act.
What equipment do you have?
Our own Sennebogen material handlers, dismantling equipment, oxy-fuel cutting equipment, containers of up to 30 m³ and tractor units with tipping trailers for collection from site.
Do you work with companies?
Yes. We offer framework contracts, containers on site, collection to a schedule and complete documentation for your accounting department.
Where are you located?
At 1 Prof. Ivan Georgov St., Voenna Rampa, Sofia. We are open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:00 Eastern European Time.
Can we visit the site before we decide?
Yes. You are welcome to see the yard, the weighbridge and the way material is accepted before you send a consignment. Please call in advance so that someone is free to show you round.

Does your operation generate metal waste?

Call us for an offer and terms for companies. We prepare an offer within 1 working day.
