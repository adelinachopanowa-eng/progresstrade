How it is formed

What the price of steel scrap depends on

There is no single price for everything made of steel. Four things move it.

Thickness. Over 6 mm the material enters the bonus grade and is bought at the highest price. Over 4 mm it is heavy melting steel. Under 3 mm it is light steel and sheet, whose price is noticeably lower.
Cleanliness. Soil, concrete, wood, plastic or insulation reduce the quantity paid for. Where the material carries non-metallic content, a deduction is applied to the weighed amount.
The market. Ferrous prices follow world exchange levels and move within the month. That is why we quote the price on the day of delivery rather than publishing a table of figures.
Quantity and transport. Larger quantities get better terms. If you deliver the material yourself you receive the full price; with our transport, the haulage is calculated into the buying price.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What is the buying price for steel scrap?
It depends on the grade. Heavy melting steel and cast iron are paid higher than light sheet, and the bonus grade and clean production offcuts are at the top. The price follows the exchange levels and is quoted for the specific consignment.
What is the difference between heavy and light steel?
Heavy melting steel is over 4 mm thick: structures, beams, profiles and machine parts. Light steel is under 3 mm: sheet metal and thin-walled parts. Heavy steel is bought at a higher price, because the losses in the furnace are smaller.
Do you collect ferrous scrap from site?
Yes. Over 2 tonnes we arrange our own transport and loading at your address in Sofia and the region, and the haulage is calculated into the buying price. Smaller batches are accepted at the yard, after a call in advance.
Do you accept turnings?
Yes, steel and cast iron turnings, provided they have been drained of cutting oil. Turnings under code 12 01 01 are accepted from legal entities only, as a condition of our permit.
How is a consignment of ferrous scrap documented?
Every consignment is weighed on a calibrated weighbridge and recorded in a waste transfer note. Companies also receive a purchase contract and a certificate of origin, and the invoice is issued under the VAT reverse charge mechanism. See the full set of documents.
