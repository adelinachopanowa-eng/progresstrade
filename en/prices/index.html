The market

Why prices change

Buying prices for ferrous scrap follow the world steel market. When the mills want more scrap, prices rise; when demand weakens, they fall.

The market moves. The difference between two periods can be substantial, without a single yard having changed the way it works.
The grade decides. Thickness and cleanliness set the grade, and the grade sets the price. Two consignments both called “steel” may fall into different grades.
Compare on the same day. Ask two places on one and the same day. Then the difference is real and it means something.

Payment

Why we pay by bank transfer only

The Bulgarian Waste Management Act allows cash payment for ferrous and non-ferrous metal waste only up to €613.55 in total per calendar year. That is why we pay only by bank transfer.

What the law says. Article 133(3)(5) of the Waste Management Act: a fine of €715.81 to €2,045.17 for a private person who has received payment in cash. It applies to every licensed yard.
The limit is for the whole year. The €613.55 is a total for the whole calendar year, not per consignment. So there are no exceptions here, whatever the quantity.
What to bring. An identity document and a bank account in the name of the person or company handing over the material, because the documents are issued in the same name. We transfer immediately after weighing.
For companies. The invoice is issued under the VAT reverse charge mechanism of Article 163a of the Bulgarian VAT Act, and payment terms can be written into a framework contract.
Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Why is the price lower than last time?
The metals market moves continuously. A price from a few months ago reflects the market as it was then, not the market today.
How do I compare you with another yard?
Ask both places on the same day. A price from weeks ago is no use for comparison, because the market has moved in the meantime.
Why is the price only settled at the yard?
The grade depends on the thickness and the cleanliness of the material, and both are visible during grading. We give an indicative price over the phone as well, if you describe what you have.
Can you pay me in cash?
No. The law allows cash payment of up to €613.55 in total for an entire calendar year, not per consignment. We pay by bank transfer immediately after weighing.
In which currency do you pay?
In euro, by transfer to a bank account in the name of the person or company handing over the material. The documents are issued in the same name.
Do larger quantities get a better price?
Yes. Larger and regular quantities carry better terms, and for a regular flow we agree the price separately, together with the container and the collection schedule.
