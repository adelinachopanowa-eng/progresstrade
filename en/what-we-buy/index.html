What we buy: ferrous and non-ferrous scrap

From heavy steel and cast iron to copper, aluminium, stainless steel and cable. Bought in batches, graded and weighed on a calibrated weighbridge, paid by bank transfer.

Materials

Fifteen grades, each priced on its own

Every grade is assessed and paid separately. A mixed consignment is separated on arrival, because brass paid as aluminium is your loss. Ferrous grades follow the ferrous price list; non-ferrous metals follow the daily exchange quotation.

Steel scrap over 6 mm thick. The top grade in the price list.

Heavy melting steel

Orientation

How the grade is decided

Thickness and cleanliness decide which grade the material falls into, and the grade decides the price.

Over 6 mm Bonus gradeOver 4 mm Heavy melting steelUnder 3 mm Light steel and sheet

The bonus grade is the highest price in the list: clean carbon steel over 6 mm, without paint or coating. Heavy melting steel covers structures, beams, profiles and machine parts. Light steel is roofing sheet, thin-walled bodies and tinplate, and it is paid noticeably lower, because the losses in the furnace are the largest.

Weighing

How the quantity is calculated

Mixed consignments. Light and heavy steel are weighed separately and each is paid at its own price. If the material cannot be separated, the ratio is determined during grading, so it is worth separating the groups while loading.
Non-metallic content. A deduction is applied to the weight. It does not apply to white goods, car bodies and end-of-life vehicles, where non-metal is part of the material, unless the contamination goes beyond the usual.
Oversized pieces. The standard limit is 500 kg and dimensions of 1.5 × 0.5 × 0.5 m. Larger pieces are bought, but the cutting is deducted from the price. Car bodies are accepted whole.
Outside our scope

What we do not accept

Not accepted. Pressure vessels in one piece, including gas and oxygen cylinders. Batteries, tyres, glass and seats. Household waste and construction waste mixed with metal.
Accepted after preparation. Tanks and vessels, once they have been cleaned of fuel and cut open.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

How do I know which grade my metal falls into?
The grade is set by the thickness of the material. Over 4 mm is heavy melting steel, under 3 mm is light steel, and over 6 mm goes into the bonus grade. If you are not sure, describe the material over the phone or show it on delivery.
What happens if I bring mixed material?
If the light and the heavy steel can be separated, two separate weighings are made and each group is paid at its own price. If the material is too thoroughly mixed, the ratio is determined during grading at the yard.
Do you accept pieces over 500 kg?
Yes. Larger pieces are bought, but the cutting work is deducted from the price, and the deduction is set according to the particular material. Car bodies are accepted whole.
Is there a minimum quantity?
Yes. We buy in batches only: from 1 tonne of ferrous scrap and from 300 kg of non-ferrous scrap. Single appliances and household metal waste are not accepted. See the quantities we work with.
Can you pay me in cash?
No. The Bulgarian Waste Management Act limits cash payment for metal waste to BGN 100 per calendar year in total, so we pay by bank transfer. Bring an identity document and a bank account in the name of the person or company handing over the material. Why we pay by bank transfer only.
Do you buy from private persons?
Yes, from private persons and from companies alike, but in batches only. Six of the non-ferrous codes in our permit may be accepted from legal entities only, which is a condition of the permit rather than a choice of ours.
