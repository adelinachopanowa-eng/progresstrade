Materials

Buying scrap metal

We buy every grade of ferrous and non-ferrous scrap: heavy and light steel, cast iron, sheet, production offcuts, turnings, copper, aluminium, stainless steel, brass, cable, old appliances and end-of-life vehicles. Material is graded and weighed on a calibrated weighbridge and paid by bank transfer.

At our yard. Voenna Rampa, with a calibrated weighbridge and full documentation. Delivery is arranged in advance, so that the weighbridge is free when you arrive.
Collection from your address. A grab truck on your site, loading and removal. Over 2 tonnes the haulage is calculated into the buying price.
Grading in front of you. The grade is determined at the point of acceptance, with the quantity per grade written into the weighing note.

On your site

Cutting and dismantling on site

Oxy-fuel cutting and dismantling of structures, machinery, plant, tanks and vessels, directly on site, for the cases where they cannot be transported in one piece.

Steel structures. Halls, silos, towers, pipe racks and reinforced concrete reinforcement after demolition.
Machinery and plant. Production lines, presses, boilers and compressors, cut down to transportable pieces.
Tanks and vessels. Accepted once they have been cleaned of fuel; the cutting is done by us.
Outside working hours. Where the site cannot stop during the day, we work to its schedule.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

How soon do you come out after an enquiry?
After a site visit or a description over the phone we agree a day. Where the quantities are regular, collection runs to a schedule agreed in advance.
What size are the containers?
Up to 30 m³. We choose the size according to the volume of the material and the access to your site.
Is there a charge for the container?
No. The container is free of charge: there is no delivery fee and no rent. We leave it on site and empty it to the agreed schedule.
Do you cut structures and machinery on site?
Yes. We carry out oxy-fuel cutting of structures, tanks and machinery directly on site, including outside working hours where the site requires it.
Which areas do you serve?
Sofia and the surrounding region. For larger quantities we arrange transport beyond that as well.
Can you handle a complete site clearance?
Yes. Valuation, dismantling, cutting, loading, removal and the documents are all done by us, so you deal with one contractor rather than four.

Tell us what you have. We take care of the rest.

Describe the material and where it is. We reply within the working day.
