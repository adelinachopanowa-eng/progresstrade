Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Do you leave a container on site?
Where the quantities are regular, we leave a container of up to 30 m³ and empty it to an agreed schedule. The container itself is free: there is no delivery charge and no rent.
Do you cut structures on site?
Yes. We cut and dismantle bulky structures, machinery, tanks and vessels directly on site, including outside working hours when the site requires it.
Is there a minimum quantity?
Yes. From 1 tonne of ferrous scrap and from 300 kg of non-ferrous scrap. For a one-off quantity we carry out a site visit and prepare an offer; for a regular flow we leave a container.
When and how is payment made?
By bank transfer, after weighing on a calibrated weighbridge. Payment terms are agreed in advance, and for a framework contract they are written into it.
Is a contract required?
There is no such requirement. For regular work, however, we can prepare a framework contract for your convenience, which also simplifies your waste register.
How does VAT work?
For companies registered for VAT in Bulgaria, the reverse charge mechanism under Article 163a of the Bulgarian VAT Act applies: you issue the invoice to us without VAT charged, and we self-assess the tax. We help with the paperwork.
Can you work with a foreign company that has no Bulgarian VAT registration?
Yes, but the treatment of the transaction differs and we agree it in advance with your accounting department. Tell us how the company is registered when you request the quote, so that the documents are right the first time.

Shall we take on your metal waste?

Tell us what metal comes out of your operations. We will propose terms for the whole flow.
