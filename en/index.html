Process

How a consignment is handled

01

Quote

You describe the material and the approximate quantity. You receive a price by phone, by email or after a visit to your site.

02

Delivery or collection

Solutions for business
Working with us from abroad

A licensed counterparty, documented to the letter

A foreign company selling metal waste in Bulgaria needs more than a price. It needs a counterparty whose permit, weighing and paperwork stand up to an audit. That is the part we have been doing since 2002.

Licensed treatment site. Waste permit No 12-DO-1270-01, issued by the Regional Inspectorate of Environment and Water in Sofia, plus a registration document for waste transport. We give you the numbers for your own compliance file.
VAT handled correctly. For companies registered for VAT in Bulgaria, the reverse charge under Article 163a of the VAT Act applies. We prepare the documents and your accounting department receives a clean set.
Weighing you can verify. Every consignment crosses a calibrated weighbridge in front of your driver or representative, and the weighing note states the quantity per grade.
Truck and rail access. The yard takes heavy goods vehicles and rail wagons, and loading is done with our own material handlers.
English throughout. Enquiries, quotes and correspondence in English, by phone, email or Viber. Buyers and traders outside Bulgaria are welcome to contact us about prepared material.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Do you buy scrap from foreign companies?
Yes. We work with Bulgarian branches and subsidiaries of foreign companies, with international contractors running projects here, and with traders abroad. Correspondence, quotes and documents are available in English, and payment is made by bank transfer to a company account.
Is there a minimum quantity?
Yes, we buy in batches only: from 1 tonne of ferrous scrap and from 300 kg of non-ferrous scrap. Over 2 tonnes we collect from your site. Single appliances and household metal waste are not accepted. See the quantities we work with.
How is the price set?
The price depends on the grade, the cleanliness and the quantity of the material, and it is quoted for the specific consignment against the exchange quotation on the day. Published figures would be out of date before they reached you, so we give the price by phone or after a site visit. How prices are formed.
How and when are you paid?
Payment is made by bank transfer, immediately after weighing on a calibrated weighbridge. The Bulgarian Waste Management Act allows cash payment for metal waste only up to BGN 100 per calendar year in total, which makes cash payment impractical for any real consignment.
What about VAT?
For companies registered for VAT in Bulgaria, the reverse charge mechanism under Article 163a of the Bulgarian VAT Act applies to waste metal: the invoice is issued without VAT charged and the recipient self-assesses the tax. We help with the paperwork.
Do you collect from site?
Yes. Over 2 tonnes we send our own grab truck and loading equipment to your address in Sofia and the region, and the haulage is calculated into the buying price. If you deliver the material to our yard yourself, you receive the higher price.
Do you cut and dismantle on site?
Yes. We carry out oxy-fuel cutting and dismantling of structures, machinery, tanks and vessels directly on site, when they cannot be transported in one piece. More about cutting and dismantling.
What are your opening hours?
The yard is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00 Eastern European Time. The address is 1 Prof. Ivan Georgov St., Voenna Rampa, Sofia. Please call before you set off, so that the weighbridge is free when you arrive.
