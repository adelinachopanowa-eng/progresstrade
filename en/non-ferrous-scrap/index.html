Materials

Six classes of non-ferrous metal

Every class is assessed and paid separately. A mixed consignment is separated on arrival, because brass paid as aluminium is your loss. Ferrous grades follow their own list; non-ferrous metals follow the daily quotation.

Copper

Busbar, tube, cable conductor and radiators. The highest price among the non-ferrous grades.

Aluminium

Profiles, sheet, castings, radiators and wheels.

Stainless steel

Grades 304 and 316 — sheet, tube, tanks and vessels.

Brass and bronze

Valves, fittings, bushings, bearings and pump bodies.

Cable

Power and signal cable, with the insulation on. Do not strip it and do not burn it.

Electric motors

Complete motors, stators and copper windings from pumps and machinery.

Pricing

Why the price is on request

Non-ferrous metals are traded on the exchange and their quotation moves every day. A published figure would be out of date before it reached you, so we quote the price by phone, on the day of the transaction.

Exchange quotation. Copper, aluminium and stainless steel follow the world exchange. The price you hear today applies to today's transaction.
Sample and cleanliness. Clean copper busbar and mixed copper are two different materials. Plastic, steel reinforcement and oil are deducted.
Form and preparation. A complete electric motor and an extracted winding are not the same thing. The less work is left for us, the higher the price for you.
Cable yield. The copper in a cable is between 5 % and 60 % of its weight, depending on the type and the cross-section. That ratio is called the yield, and it is what the price is based on, because the metal is paid for rather than the gross weight.

The figures above are indicative. The exact yield is measured when the consignment is accepted.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

What is the price of non-ferrous scrap?
The price follows the exchange quotations and is set after inspection and sampling, which is why we do not publish it: a figure from yesterday would mislead you today. Call us on +359 877 77 55 77 and you will get a range straight away, and the exact price after weighing and sampling.
Is there a minimum quantity?
Yes, from 300 kg for non-ferrous scrap. For ferrous scrap the threshold is 1 tonne. See the quantities we work with.
Do I need to strip the cable?
No. We accept cable with the insulation on, and the copper yield is determined by the cross-section and the type. Burning insulation is prohibited by law and it destroys part of the value of the copper.
Why is burnt copper paid lower?
Burnt copper gives a larger loss when it is remelted, so the refinery pays less for it. The difference reaches the seller as a lower price per kilogram, which is why open burning costs you money as well as breaking the law.
Do you buy non-ferrous scrap from private persons?
Only in significant quantities. Six of the non-ferrous codes in our permit may be accepted from legal entities only, so for those we work with companies alone. Single items and household metal waste are not bought.
What documents do you issue?
All the documents required by the Bulgarian Waste Management Act: a waste transfer note, and for companies an invoice under the VAT reverse charge mechanism. We operate under waste permit No 12-DO-1270-01.
