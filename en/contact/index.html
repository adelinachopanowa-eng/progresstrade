Arriving by lorry: how a handover works

1. Call before you set off. This matters: we confirm the material, the price and the time slot on +359 877 77 55 77.
2. Weighing at the gate. The lorry crosses the calibrated weighbridge.
3. Unloading. With our own material handler, quickly and without waiting.
4. Documents and payment. We prepare the documents on site and pay straight away.

For oversized loads and articulated combinations, please call +359 877 77 55 77 in advance, so that we can arrange priority unloading.

Area served

A scrap yard for Sofia and the whole region

Our yard is in the Voenna Rampa district, minutes from Nadezhda, Iliyantsi, the freight station and Sofia Central Station. We accept material from every district of Sofia and from the surrounding region, including Bozhurishte, Kostinbrod and Novi Iskar. For larger quantities we arrange collection from your address.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to call before I come?
Yes. Delivery to the yard is by prior arrangement on +359 877 77 55 77, so that the weighbridge is free and the price is agreed before you set off.
What are the opening hours?
Monday to Friday, from 9:00 to 17:00 Eastern European Time. Outside those hours you can send a photograph on Viber or fill in the form, and we will prepare an offer within 1 working day.
What documents should I bring?
Private persons bring an identity document and a bank account number in their own name. For companies we prepare the contract and the waste transfer note on site.
Where exactly is the yard?
In Sofia, in the Voenna Rampa district, at 1 Prof. Ivan Georgov St. The entrance is from the yard side, with access for heavy goods vehicles.
Do you answer enquiries in English?
Yes. Phone, Viber and email enquiries are handled in English, and the documents for a transaction can be prepared in English alongside the Bulgarian originals.
